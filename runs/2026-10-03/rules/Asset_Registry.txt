RENDERER / POST-PRODUCTION TOOL CANDIDATES — CURRENT
• MiniMax H3 local ComfyUI workflow — ACTIVE / TESTING. An observed step-zero crash at 720 × 1280 was traced to incompatible latent geometry: the width becomes 45 after the current downsampling path, which is odd for 2 × 2 spatial patchification. Preferred next test: 736 × 1312 for more detail and VRAM headroom; lower-load fallback: 576 × 1024. Do not treat 800 × 1424 as safe under the same rule because 1424 ÷ 16 = 89, also odd.
• LTX-2.5 by Lightricks — CANDIDATE, not selected. It is an open-weights audio-video model with image-to-video, audio-to-video and native multishot capabilities. Those features could reduce separate presenter/voice/lip-sync steps, but that benefit is unproven for this project. Official local ComfyUI guidance specifies a CUDA GPU with 32 GB+ VRAM; Rafael’s RTX 3090 has 24 GB. Third-party quantized/distilled builds exist, but operation within 24 GB is untested on Rafael’s machine.
• Wan2.2 — CANDIDATE / SECONDARY. Strong general open video family, but not currently preferred over a renderer with a more integrated presenter-audio route.
• Hallo3 — CANDIDATE / FALLBACK. Audio-driven portrait animation focused on identity consistency; local practicality and output quality for the full studio shot are untested.
• Final-video upscaler — NOT INSTALLED / CANDIDATE. A lower-resolution native vertical render followed by upscaling only after the complete bulletin is assembled is under evaluation. No upscaler is approved or installed.
• Post-production tools — ACTIVE / PARTIALLY TESTED. ffmpeg stitching, Whisper transcription and English subtitle burn-in on the i5 have been exercised. Repeatability, final visual QA and the complete automated chain remain to be verified.
