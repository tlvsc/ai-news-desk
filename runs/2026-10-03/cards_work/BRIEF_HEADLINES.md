 "fun":{"item":"C16-01","script":"...","screen":"..."},

 "bp":{"script":"...","screen":"..."}}
Script shape for a story: "In <category word>. <the deed in one plain sentence>. <one plain sentence: what it means or the honest doubt>." The category word is one or two plain words (business, security, energy, robotics, science, health, society, politics, law, models). Fun starts with "And a lighter story." Teaser starts with "Also in the full report:" and gives two items, each a short sentence. The Bigger Picture starts with "And for the bigger picture:" and says what moves the market and what to watch, in plain words.
Length: the speaking time is syllables divided by 4.4 syllables per second. Aim for 8 seconds (about 35 syllables, 30 to 38 words at most 15 words a sentence). The Bigger Picture may run up to 12 seconds (about 50 syllables). Never more than 12 seconds in any clip (53 syllables). Numbers are spoken as words ("seventy three percent", "four thousand nine hundred ninety nine dollars"); write them as words in the script. Acronyms are spaced so they are spoken letter by letter ("A I", "C E O"). Pronunciation cue: write Anthropic as "an-thropic" and Nvidia as "N-vidia" only where it is spoken. Never speak the outlet name. Keep every hedge (reportedly, says, plans, may). No second source facts: only the entry's facts.
"screen": one line for the glowing blue holographic screen behind the presenter: a small simple symbol that matches the story, text free and number free, in the 2 Oct style, for example "a small glowing robot hand reaching into an empty window frame, simple text-free symbol". It must be easy for a video model to draw.
Validate with python json.load. Final message: one line only ("headlines scripts written").
