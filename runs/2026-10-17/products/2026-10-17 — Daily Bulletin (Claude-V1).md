## ETHICS AND LAW — 2
**8 HIGH — Anthropic has shown US lawmakers a draft federal AI safety bill that would let the government block an AI model only when it poses an imminent, extreme danger, Politico reports.** (report item 75)  
Politico reports, as relayed by Reuters, that Anthropic representatives have shown Capitol Hill lawmakers a draft bill under which the government could block a frontier AI model (one of the most advanced systems) only if it posed a foreseeable, imminent risk of killing or seriously injuring more than 50 people or causing more than $1 billion in damages through a biological weapon, the model escaping its developer's controls or a cyberattack.
