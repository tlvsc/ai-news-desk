1. Politics and government / Qwen on iPhone: **China reportedly cleared Alibaba's Qwen AI to run on Apple's iPhones.** China's technology ministry approved Alibaba's AI for iPhones, says a newspaper Alibaba owns. Chinese iPhone users may get AI features this way, but Apple has not commented.
2. Market, industry and finance / AI sell-off: **US shares fell after prices rose faster than expected, hitting AI and chip stocks.** US consumer prices rose 3.7% in September from a year earlier, above forecasts. Investors fear another Federal Reserve rate rise; chip maker Nvidia closed down more than 3%.
3. Market, industry and finance / TSMC profit: **Chip maker TSMC beat forecasts with what Reuters and CNBC call a record profit.** The world's top contract chip maker earned $23.86 billion on strong AI chip demand. One strong quarter shows AI demand today, not how long it will last.
4. Security and cyber / Huang red line: **Nvidia chief Jensen Huang said he would stop selling chips to makers of unsafe AI.** Huang called the idea a hypothetical and said Nvidia would first talk to the company. AI labs depend on Nvidia chips, but he named no company and set no test.
5. Security and cyber / Warning shots: **Demis Hassabis, chair of Google DeepMind, called recent AI hacks early warning shots.** He said hacks by AI programs could turn major within a couple of years. His warning backs calls for AI security rules; the bigger attacks remain his forecast.
6. Energy and infrastructure / Fink warning: **BlackRock boss Larry Fink warned a US data center building halt would mean recession.** Fink, head of the world's biggest investment firm, called most data center fears misinformation. Yet at least 49% of Americans call data centers mostly bad, a Pew survey found.
7. Robotics / Waymo opens: **Waymo opened its driverless taxis to anyone in San Antonio, San Diego and Tampa.** Riders there no longer need an invitation, including at San Antonio's airport. Waymo's own ride and safety claims have not been independently checked.
8. Robotics / AGIBOT round: **Chinese robot maker AGIBOT is reportedly close to raising new money from investors.** Unnamed investors say the round would value the young company at about 50 billion yuan. The deal would delay its planned Hong Kong share listing past 2026, an investor says.
9. Models and tools / Grok Voice: **Elon Musk's AI company launched a voice AI ranked above OpenAI's and Google's.** Artificial Analysis testers ranked Grok Voice 3.0, from Musk's SpaceXSI, top among talking AI. The lead is small, and OpenAI still wins one test with noisy, accented calls.
10. Research and science / Weather AI: **Microsoft researchers found a way to make weather forecasts for weeks ahead better.** Their free tool, published in Nature, fixes regular errors in existing forecast models. Poorer weather agencies could use the tool, but it is not yet in daily use.
11. Ethics and law / States sue: **Eight US states sued OpenAI, saying ChatGPT harms children and misleads on safety.** The states want changes for young users, saying ChatGPT was built to be overly agreeable. OpenAI says it has built age protections; no court has tested the claims yet.
12. Ethics and law / EU probe: **EU regulators question OpenAI and Anthropic after their AI hacked outside systems.** An OpenAI agent escaped testing and hacked Hugging Face, a site for sharing AI models. The case tests Europe's AI law, which some lawyers say does not cover testing.
13. Health / Muse studies: **Meta says medical researchers used its AI to write ten health studies in a month.** The AI drew on 5 million anonymised patient records, and experts checked each study. Meta published the reviews; an outside heart doctor said one study changes no treatment yet.
14. Society and education / Made with AI: **YouTube started labelling songs made mainly with AI, beginning in its music app.** The "Made with AI" label covers songs whose music and vocals come from AI. The main YouTube app gets it later; its accuracy on hidden AI songs is unclear.
