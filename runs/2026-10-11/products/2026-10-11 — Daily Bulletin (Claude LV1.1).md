## SECURITY AND CYBER — 4
**8 HIGH — Microsoft chief executive Satya Nadella called for AI systems to be built so that a person can always pause or shut a model down mid-task.** (report item 21)  
TechCrunch reports that Microsoft chief executive Satya Nadella wrote on X on Saturday 10 Oct that AI should not be treated as 'nested black boxes' whose answers people simply accept or reject, and proposed keeping the model separate from the software that runs its work, putting safeguards outside it, recording every meaningful action as tamper-proof evidence people can read, and letting an authorised person pause or shut a model mid-task.
