1. PERMANENT ASSETS (folder: Bulletin blueprint V1 / Permanent Assets)
====================================================================
1. build_pdf.py — the PDF program. One program makes both the Daily Bulletin and the
   Full Report (--kind bulletin or --kind report). Python 3, ReportLab.
2. _bulletin_reference_input.json — the exact input that produced the approved 27 Sep
   2026 bulletin (42 stories, 10 categories). Use it to test any change to the program.
3. _bulletin_reference_file.pdf — the approved 27 Sep 2026 bulletin itself
   (Daily_Bulletin_27-9-26.pdf, 20 pages, A4, 168,897 bytes). Rafael adds this file by
   hand; the Drive connection cannot upload a PDF of this size. Every future bulletin is
   compared against it.
4. fonts — optional. The program downloads any missing font on first run (Source Serif 4
   from adobe-fonts on GitHub, Instrument Sans from google/fonts) and stops if it cannot;
   it never falls back to another font. Both families are free under the SIL Open Font
   License. Keeping the files here removes the download step.

2. LOOK
====================================================================
Page: A4 portrait, white. Margins 18 mm left and right, 18 mm top, 20 mm bottom; body
pages start 24 mm from the top to leave room for the running header.

Colours (exact hex):
- Navy #09132A: all main text, headlines, rules, masthead.
- Electric blue #3FA9F5: accent bars, the short thick accent on section rules, rank numerals.
- Blue ink #1B75BA: small blue text (date line, "SECTION nn", source links, running
  section name). It is the same hue darkened so small text stays readable on white.
- Blue tint #E8F4FE: fill of the CONFIRMED status pill.
- Amber #FFC45A and amber tint #FFF3DB: status pills for REPORTED, PRELIMINARY,
  UNCONFIRMED and DEVELOPING (tint fill, amber outline); DISPUTED is solid amber.
- Grey text #566074 (labels, coverage, captions), grey soft #8A93A3 (separators, web
  domains), grey rule #D8DEE7 (hairlines), grey tint #EEF1F5 (pill for any other status).

Fonts (embedded as subsets; the finished PDF is checked for any non-embedded font):
- Source Serif 4 for headlines and reading text: Display Semibold for the title and
  section titles, Subhead Semibold for story headlines, Regular and Italic for body text.
- Instrument Sans for navigation, labels and source lines: Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold.

Sizes in the bulletin (points, size/leading):
- Product title "Daily Bulletin": Serif Display 50/54.
- Date line: Sans Medium 14/18, blue ink.
- Purpose line: Serif Italic 14/20, indented 30 mm from the right.
- Coverage line: Sans 8.8/13, the word "Coverage" in Sans SemiBold navy.
- Section title (category name): Serif Display 25/29; above it "SECTION nn" in Sans
  SemiBold 7.8, blue ink, letter-spaced; under it "n stories · ranked by importance" in Sans 8.5.
- Story headline: Serif Subhead 16.5/20.5.
- Story text: Serif 10.8/16, 7 pt after each paragraph.
- Source line: Sans 8.4/11.
- Masthead "AI NEWS DESK": Sans Bold 11, letter-spaced, with a blue bar, over a 1.1 pt navy rule.

Front page, top to bottom: masthead, navy rule, title, date, purpose line, coverage line,
then CONTENTS (blue ink label, "n categories · n stories", a navy rule with a 28 mm blue
accent, and one linked row per category plus the extra sections, each with its page
number). In the bulletin the first category starts on the front page under the contents.

Every page: footer with a grey hairline, "AI NEWS DESK" in Sans Bold 6.8, then
"Daily Bulletin · <date>" in grey, and "Page x of y" on the right in navy.
Every page after the first: running header with a small blue bar, "AI NEWS DESK",
"Daily Bulletin", and on the right the name of the category on that page in blue ink,
over a grey hairline. The PDF opens with its bookmarks panel showing the categories.

Section header: a navy 0.8 pt rule with a 28 mm blue accent, "SECTION nn", the category
name, and the story count line. A heading never sits alone at the bottom of a page.

Story block (bulletin): single column. The story's rank inside its category hangs in a
14 mm left gutter as a blue numeral. The text column is indented 12 mm on the right to
keep lines near 80 characters. Order inside a block:
1. Headline.
2. Label row: "Importance: HIGH (8)" (label and score), "Rank n of m in category", and
   the status pill (CONFIRMED, REPORTED and so on).
3. The paragraphs (section 5.3).
4. Source line: "Source: <Publisher> ↗ · domain.com", the publisher name a live link to
   the original article.
Stories are separated by a thin divider. The headline, the label row and the first lines
of the text never split across pages, and a source line never starts a column alone.

3. STRUCTURE
====================================================================
1. Front page as above.
2. Categories in the V1 order of 23 Sep 2026, skipping any category with no bulletin story:
   Politics and government; Market, industry and finance; Security and cyber; Energy and
   infrastructure; Robotics; Models and tools; Research and science; Ethics and law;
   Health; Society and education; then The Fun Side, last, with its top 3 stories
   (Rafael, 30 Sep 2026).
3. Inside each category the highest score comes first; ties keep the Full Report order.
   Rank n of m is the story's place inside its category in the Full Report.
4. After the categories: "About this edition" (section 7).

====================================================================
4. SELECTION
====================================================================
1. Source is the Full Report of the same day only. No web-only story is added. Facts,
   scores, statuses and sources are copied unchanged; a missing story is fixed in the
   Full Report first.
2. Rule (Rafael, 28 Sep 2026, CLAUDE.md rule 14): the score cutoff flexes with the day's
   pool so the bulletin lands near 30 to 50 stories (usually 6, 7 or 8 and up); every Full
   Report story at or above that cutoff goes in, grouped by category, most important first.
   State the cutoff and the count. The Fun Side does not count toward the cutoff: its top 3
   stories are added last (Rafael, 30 Sep 2026). This replaces the 27 Sep 2026 rule "every
   story scored 7 to 10".
3. Counts are guidelines, not gates (CLAUDE.md rule 14): the number follows the day's
   pool; state the number, never stop to ask about it. 27 Sep 2026 had 42 stories.
4. Score bands: 10 CRITICAL, 8 and 9 HIGH, 6 and 7 MEDIUM, 1 to 5 WATCHLIST.

====================================================================
5. WRITING
====================================================================
5.1 Reader and phrasing: use the shared rules and the Bulletin section of
    Article_phrasing_instructions_AIND_V1; that file alone defines the reader level.
5.2 Meaning check (Plain Language Law section 1A, approved 26 Sep 2026) on every bulletin
    story: read the full source, shorten, compare back, rewrite if a fact, consequence or
    qualification went missing or a stronger certainty appeared. Keep every hedge and
    every attribution ("Bloomberg reports"). A possibility is never written as a fact.
5.3 Story format (copied from the LV1.1 report story format with Rafael's approval,
    27 Sep 2026):
    - Headline: one full sentence saying what happened, not leading with an unexplained
      acronym or product name.
    - Importance label and score, rank, status (shown in the label row).
    - Paragraph 1, the summary: one or two complete sentences; the first is the event
      with its attribution, the next gives the practical "why it matters" or new facts.
    - Paragraph 2: "Why it matters: " plus one sentence naming what changed, who it
      affects (people first, then institutions, then companies) and the honest doubt.
    - Paragraph 3, only when the original article could not be opened: "Note: we could
      not open the original article; this entry relies on its headline and related
      coverage."
    - The headline and summary may repeat the Full Report word for word; the Plain
      Language Law's "never lift a sentence" rule covers cards and clips, not the bulletin.
5.4 No sales language, no buy or sell advice, no marketing words.

====================================================================
6. CREDIT AND LINKS
====================================================================
1. Every story names its publisher and links to the original article, never to a
   Google News or other redirect link. Credit to the people who reported the story is
   required; this is why the link stays even on headline-only stories.
2. The source name is the publisher only. Internal notes ("via", "(blocked)", research
   remarks) are removed before printing; a long name is cut at its first comma.

====================================================================
7. FIXED TEXT
====================================================================
- Title: Daily Bulletin. Date: "<day> <Month> <year>", for example 27 September 2026.
- Purpose line: "A concise selection of the day's most important AI developments, taken
  from the Full Report: every story scored <cutoff> or more, plus the top 3 from The Fun
  Side."
- Coverage line: "<reporting window> · <n> stories".
- About this edition, three short paragraphs: where the stories were collected and for
  which window; how many full articles were read and how many are headline only; the
  score scale and the line "Nothing here is investment advice."

====================================================================
8. INPUT FORMAT (what build_pdf.py reads)
====================================================================
{"title": "Daily Bulletin", "date": "27 September 2026", "purpose": "...", "coverage": "...",
 "categories": [{"name": "Politics and government",
                 "items": [{"headline", "score", "importance", "rank", "rank_of", "status",
                            "paragraphs": [summary, "Why it matters: ...", optional note],
                            "source", "url"}]}],
 "extra_sections": [{"title": "About this edition", "paragraphs": [...]}]}
Any other key is ignored and never printed. _bulletin_reference_input.json is a complete
real example.

====================================================================
9. REBUILD
====================================================================
1. Put build_pdf.py in a folder (with a fonts subfolder if the font files are kept).
2. Build the input JSON from the day's verified Full Report entries (sections 4, 5, 8).
3. Run: python build_pdf.py --input bulletin_input.json --kind bulletin --out Daily_Bulletin_D-M-YY.pdf
4. The program installs ReportLab if missing, renders twice so "Page x of y" and the
   contents page numbers are right, and checks that every font is embedded.
5. Test after any change to the program: build from _bulletin_reference_input.json and
   compare page by page with _bulletin_reference_file.pdf.

11. FINAL CHECK BEFORE SHIPPING
====================================================================
1. Every story comes from today's Full Report; scores, statuses and sources unchanged.
2. Every story at or above the day's cutoff is present, in category order, highest first,
   and the top 3 Fun Side stories come last.
3. Every story has a publisher name and a working direct link.
4. Meaning check passed on every story; headline-only stories carry the note.
5. Page count, contents page numbers and "Page x of y" agree; every font is embedded.
6. Spot-check the first page and two story pages against the reference PDF.
7. Publication needs Rafael's explicit approval.
