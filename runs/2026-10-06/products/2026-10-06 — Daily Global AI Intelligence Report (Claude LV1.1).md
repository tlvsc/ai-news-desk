*What today's developments could mean for technology, business and everyday life*

### 1. What AI can do now

Fact: TechCrunch reports that Reflection AI, a two-year-old start-up backed by Nvidia, unveiled Beam, an open-weight model (one whose trained parameters are to be published so others can run it), which the company says matches the Chinese developer Z.ai's GLM-5.2 on advanced reasoning tests while needing three to four times less computing power than rival models. The claims are the company's own and not independently verified (item 83).
