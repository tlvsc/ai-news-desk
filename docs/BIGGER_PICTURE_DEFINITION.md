# The Bigger Picture: what it must be

Rafi asked on 17 Oct 2026 for one page that collects everything he said about the Bigger Picture, so it is all in front of us when we work on it.
His words are quoted exactly; the short lines around them are Claude's. The rules themselves stay in CLAUDE.md (rules 17, 38, 40, 43 and BIGGER PICTURE WEIGHT); this page only gathers them.

## 1. Why it matters

- "If I want to give a product for investors to save them money in doing the work themselves, I need to give them a really good product."
- "I want to give value for my customers ... it's not a financial advice, but to try to predict or explain where everything is heading."
- "I want my customers to be able to find the next Nvidia, to be able to find the next Micron, or the next upcoming either trends or demands."
- "This is not the product I want to give my customers." (17 Oct, about a Bigger Picture that repeated the news.)

## 2. What it is

- 2 Oct: "a summation of all the big things happening, focused on what moves the market, followed by things to watch and follow so the story can be seen unfolding. It is never a vague restatement of the day's headlines." (rule 17)
- 7 Oct: "a meaningful analysis with a meaningful thing to watch" in every product, with scheduled events and their dates and sources, and the line "This is not financial advice." (BIGGER PICTURE WEIGHT)
- 17 Oct: "The bigger picture should summarize what is happening in the field ... We need to give an investor a clear investor tips and where the market is heading. So what is happening in the market and the conclusions."
- 17 Oct: "one page that's called Investor Watch or the bigger picture, and that should have a real meaningful effect of what is happening in the market in the next week, in the next month, in the next year, or in the next 3 years."

## 3. The example he gave

- "About a month ago, a big thing happened when magnets started going up because robotics need magnets. We could have had this as a big analysis of things."
- How to find such things: "if we calculate and we count all our pool articles, or we look at the past of our items, and we can get some information from that, not only from them ... connecting all the threads, what is happening throughout all the items around ... And then predict the future, give it chances."

## 4. Trends, not predictions; no company names

- "We do not give prediction, but trend is not a prediction. If we give an item saying it would be important and there's big chances that memory will rise because demands from this and this and this happening, then we don't give names of companies, we just give what's happening, and that's good enough."
- "We can put charts if needed."

## 5. The shape

- Length, 17 Oct: "one page at maximum ... not like yesterday it was 12,000 words, about six pages of useless. It was good information, but not necessarily correct as a bigger picture."
- Key points, 17 Oct: "a list of key points of where the market is heading ... let's say 5 key points ... upcoming weeks or upcoming months or upcoming years, those points should be as points, even with estimations of how things can happen and what are the chances."
- Use of the points: "1 point for the headline, 1 point for the card, 2 points of the summarization for the bulletin, and then the rest will appear in the full report."
- Weekly: "every week on Friday, give a big final report, like a weekly report."
- Repeats: "it's okay to repeat points during the week ... we can point it out that this point is coming up again. And that kind of gives people the idea of things happening."
- Bulletin version: "300-500 words is fine for now"; "stick to whats necessary" (13 Oct).
